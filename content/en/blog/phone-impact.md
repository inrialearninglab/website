In a world where technology evolves at lightning speed, it's easy to think only of innovation and forget what it costs the planet. "Phone Impact" immerses you in this reality by putting you in the role of a smartphone manufacturer in TechCity, where competition is fierce to produce the most innovative phones.

But one crucial question remains: What is the environmental and social impact of producing a smartphone?

## What is Phone Impact?

Phone Impact is much more than just a game. Here is what makes it unique:

- A serious game co-designed by learning designers and scientific experts from Inria, combining scientific rigor with a playful approach.
- Essential awareness-raising: it highlights the environmental and social impacts of the extractive activities required to manufacture smartphones.
- A collective and interactive experience, where every decision counts.
- Ready-to-use [resources](https://learninglab.gitlabpages.inria.fr/serious-game/smartphone/Ressources/Introduction.html){target="_blank"} to support your experience, freely available tools to guide you from the introduction to the final debrief.

## Why choose Phone Impact?

Because it combines learning with fun, individual and group thinking, and above all, it engages participants in a dialogue. Whether you're a player, a facilitator, or simply curious, you'll find all the resources you need to discover, play, and facilitate Phone Impact.

Because learning through play means taking the issues seriously.
