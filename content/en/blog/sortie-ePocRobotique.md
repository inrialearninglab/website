The Learning Lab team is launching a new free mobile training course dedicated to a rapidly growing field: manipulation robotics. Accessible to everyone, this program offers a clear and progressive introduction to the essential foundations of modern robotics.  

## Why take an interest in robotics today?  
Robotics is already transforming many sectors — industry, healthcare, logistics — and its evolution is accelerating. Understanding its foundations is becoming a real asset, whether you are a student, a professional, or simply curious about emerging technologies.  

## A complete, accessible and interactive training course
Entitled "Fundamentals of Manipulation Robotics", this training guides you through the key concepts:    
    • The historical evolution of robotics.   
    • The essential components of robotic systems.   
    • The different movement methods.   
    • Perception mechanisms.   
    • The role of artificial intelligence.   

The content has been designed for smooth and dynamic mobile learning, with:  
    • short and educational videos,  
    • interactive animations,  
    • quizzes to test your knowledge,  
    • and scientifically validated content by Jean-Pierre Merlet, Inria researcher, and Clara Thomas, Inria/3IA PhD candidate.  

## What you will learn
At the end of this training, you will be able to:    
✔ Understand the origins and major milestones of robotics.   
✔ Identify the different robot movement strategies.   
✔ Understand the basics of robotic perception.   
✔ Explore artificial intelligence applications in robotics.     
✔ Analyze technological challenges and associated ethical issues.   

## How to access the training?
Nothing could be easier:  
    • Download the ePoc application: https://epoc.inria.fr/epocs/e013jm   
    • Search for the training in the catalog: "Fundamentals of Manipulation Robotics"  
Start learning at your own pace, directly on your mobile device.
